## Signing in

Click the circular avatar at the bottom of the activity bar (left edge of the window). The panel shows your name and email if you are already signed in, or a **Sign in** button if you are not.

Click **Sign in**. Your default browser opens to the Thrindex sign-in page. Enter your credentials. When sign-in completes, the browser shows "Opening Thrindex" and returns control to the app. The avatar updates to show your initials.

The app caches your session securely using the macOS credential store. On subsequent launches, the session is refreshed automatically in the background - you do not need to sign in again unless you explicitly sign out or your session expires from inactivity.

---

## What sign-in unlocks

| Feature | Without sign-in | With sign-in |
|---|---|---|
| Open and inspect projects | ✓ | ✓ |
| Browse runs and files | ✓ | ✓ |
| Install datasets | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Train** | Blocked | ✓ |

---

## Account panel

The account panel shows:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Your display name |
| **Email** | Your account email address |
| **Organization** | Your organization on the account site |
| **Plan** | Your current plan |

**Manage account** opens the account page on the Thrindex website in your browser, where you can change your password, enable two-factor authentication, and manage active sessions.

**Sign out** revokes the desktop session on the server and clears the local cache. The next training attempt will show "Sign in to train."

---

## Feedback

Click **Feedback** in the top bar to open the feedback dialog.

Select a type:
- **Bug** - something is broken or behaves unexpectedly
- **Suggestion** - a feature or improvement you would like to see
- **Question** - something you are unsure about

Write your message and click **Send** (or press **⌘ Return**). The app sends the message along with your platform, app version, device, and email (if signed in).

Your feedback goes directly to the team. Anonymous submissions are accepted.

---

## Session expiry

If you do not use the app for an extended period, your session may expire. When this happens, the app shows "Session expired. Sign in again." in the Train button tooltip. Click the account avatar and sign in to resume.
