<Warning>E0201: backend could not parse artifact</Warning>

## What happened

The simulator could not load a `.thx` artifact. The JSON could not be parsed, or the layers could not be turned into a runnable model.

## Why

Possible causes:
- The artifact is corrupt (see [E0008](/errors/E0008), [E0009](/errors/E0009))
- The file was not produced by this version of `thx.compile`

## How to fix

1. **Recompile** with `thx.compile(model, "model.thx")`.
2. **Verify the artifact is valid**: `thrindex doctor --check model.thx`.

## Example

```
E0201: backend could not parse artifact
Why: {detail}
Fix: verify the artifact was produced by `thrindex compile` and is not corrupted
Docs: https://docs.thrindex.com/errors/E0201
```
